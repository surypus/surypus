aggregateMetrics :: [MetricPoint] -> Text -> Either Text Double Source #

Aggregate metrics

avg over an empty series has no defined value: dividing by zero yields NaN, which compares false against everything and would silently poison every threshold check downstream. Reject it instead. sum and count are well-defined over the empty series (0) and stay total.
