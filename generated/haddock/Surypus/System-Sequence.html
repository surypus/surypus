nextNumber :: Sequence -> Text Source #

Generate next number.

The increment is the sequence's configured seqStep (not a hardcoded 1): a sequence configured with a step of 5 must yield 5, 10, 15…, not 1, 2, 3…. The step is applied as-is and may be negative; a step of 0 yields no movement. seqFlags carries no defined bits anywhere in the codebase and is deliberately ignored here.
