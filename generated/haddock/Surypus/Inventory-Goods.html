goodsForRestocking :: [Goods] -> [Stock] -> [Goods] Source #

Goods that are below their minimum stock level.

Available quantity is on-hand minus reserved, summed across every location. A good is reported when it is active, has a minimum-stock policy, and its available quantity is below that minimum. Goods with no minimum policy are never reported, so a zero gMinStock means "no restocking policy" rather than "always restock".
